Care for the blue

Healthy oceans need all of us

Small, steady actions can reduce pressure on marine habitats. Start where you are, choose what feels manageable, and keep learning about the issues that matter in your community.

Cut down on waste

Carry a reusable bottle and bag, skip unnecessary single-use packaging, and sort waste according to local recycling rules. Keep litter secured so it cannot blow into storm drains and waterways.

Choose ocean-friendly meals

Ask where seafood comes from and how it was caught or farmed. Use a trusted regional seafood guide, try plant-forward meals, and avoid products whose origin cannot be clearly explained.

Give wildlife room

Watch marine animals quietly from a distance. Never feed, chase, touch, or block their path; follow local viewing rules and leave shells, coral, and other natural finds where they belong.

Help habitats and research

Join a well-organized beach or river cleanup, support reputable conservation groups, and take part in local wildlife counts or community science projects. Share reliable information, not wildlife locations that could put animals at risk.
